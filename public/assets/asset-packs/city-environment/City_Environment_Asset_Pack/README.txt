CITY / URBAN ENVIRONMENT ASSET PACK
====================================

Created from the supplied city background and asset-sheet images.

Folders:
01_background       City panorama
02_buildings_landmarks  Landmark towers and buildings
03_bridges_roads    Bridges and road/river pieces
04_trees_vegetation Trees
05_vehicles         Vehicles
06_street_props     Lamps, signs, traffic objects, barriers, benches and pedestrians
00_reference        Original supplied images

Each asset is exported as an individual PNG. Crops use transparent-capable
RGBA PNG files; source art is preserved in 00_reference.
